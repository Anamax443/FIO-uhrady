The problem it solves

Running costs for a property consist of dozens of items with different frequencies — monthly advances, quarterly insurance, annual tax, one-off repairs. Splitting them between several participants, tracking who has paid what, and reconciling once a year takes hours in a spreadsheet every year, and produces an argument every time someone asks "how did you arrive at that?".

The application runs this administration on its own: it records costs, allocates them to individual people, downloads transactions from the bank account and matches them against what is owed, tracks the running balance, and performs the monthly closing and the settlement without operator involvement.

Settlementadvances vs. reality
The key principle: a participant pays a fixed advance by standing order — nothing to change month to month. The difference against real costs is reconciled once per period and folded into the new advance, exactly the way utility suppliers work. The system therefore needs a single decision per year.

Benefits

  • Unattended operation — closings and the annual settlement run themselves; the operator only approves or corrects.
  • Defensible numbers — every amount shows where it came from; every change has a name and a timestamp.
  • No arguments — each participant has their own overview with the breakdown, including a QR payment.
  • Outage tolerance — bank data is fetched for the whole window, so an outage catches up by itself.

Risks and how they are handled

  • Unidentified payment — unmatched transactions are never discarded; they wait for manual assignment.
  • Retroactive change — a closed month is frozen and automation will not overwrite it.
  • Access to data — administration requires sign-in; a participant sees only their own figures.
  • Dependence on the bank — access is read-only; the system cannot send a payment.

Technical solution and cost

The application runs serverless on the Cloudflare platform (Workers plus the D1 database), so there is no server to operate, back up or patch. Operation fits within the free tier; the only external dependency is the bank API with read-only access. Deploying a new version takes tens of seconds and is reversible.
