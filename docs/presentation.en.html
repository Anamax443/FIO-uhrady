FIO-uhrady — information flow

How data travels from the bank to the person who owes money. As of 21 Aug 2026.

inputs — where data comes from processing inside the application outputs — who looks at what

The four steps it is made of

1Downloading transactions Every 15 minutes the whole window is fetched, not "since last time". If a write failed, the next run fetches the same data again — a transaction cannot be lost. Duplicates are caught by the bank's transaction id.
2Matching payments The variable symbol is the primary marker, so a contribution can arrive from anywhere. When it is missing, a known symbol is looked for in the transaction comment and in the message for the recipient. Anything unrecognised waits for the administrator — and every payment shows how it was matched.
3Splitting the costs Each item is split across named people, by percentage or by a fixed amount. The combination differs per item; there are no fixed groups. A purchase can be spread over months, because coal worth 42,000 is not one month's cost.
4Automation A month closes itself on the due date of the following month, and once the period is complete the settlement runs on its own. Finished work is never overwritten, and the administrator can undo and redo both.

Why a fixed advance and a settlement

Real costs fluctuate — coal in winter, nothing in summer, property tax once a year. Paying exactly what they are would mean changing the standing order every month. So a fixed advance is paid and the difference against reality is reconciled once per period, the same way utilities work.

TermWhat it means
AdvanceA fixed monthly amount paid by standing order. Its history is never rewritten, so a retrospective calculation matches what was really being paid at the time.
ClosingFreezes what applied in that month — costs, shares and the advance. Without it, hindsight would claim January's figure was today's.
SettlementReconciles advances against reality for the whole period. The difference is folded into the new advance so the standing order stays fixed.
Running balanceThe debt carries from month to month. If one month falls short, the missing part is added to the next.

The principles holding it together

No write without an audit recordThe change and its record go in one batch. A change without a visible who and when cannot exist.
Never overwrite finished workAutomation will not touch a closed month or a completed settlement. Otherwise closings would be meaningless.
Wording belongs in settingsAnything that may change — the QR recipient name, the sentences members read — is written in Settings, not in the code.
Output must make sense from outsideA message must reveal whether the system is waiting, switched off, or broken — and what to do about it.
